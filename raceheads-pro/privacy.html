Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Package com.dagli.raceheads.pro

  1. Who we are

    RaceHeads Pro is the paid, ad-free edition of the RaceHeads racing game for Android, developed and published by Mehmet Dagli (solo developer).

    Contact: mehmt.dagli@gmail.com

  2. Scope

    This policy applies to the RaceHeads Pro Android app (package com.dagli.raceheads.pro). The free RaceHeads app has its own policy.

  3. No data collected

    RaceHeads Pro does not collect, transmit or share any personal or device data. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, photos, contacts or any account information. The game has no user accounts, no chat and no online features; it plays offline.

  4. No ads, no third-party SDKs

    RaceHeads Pro shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. The app does not use the Android advertising ID. There are no in-app purchases.

  5. Data stored on your device

    Your settings and game progress (selected car and circuit, cup results, medals, personal best lap times and your car paint choices) are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

  6. Google Play

    The app is purchased and distributed through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. Google may collect crash and performance diagnostics (Android vitals) under the Google Privacy Policy.

  7. Children's privacy

    RaceHeads Pro is suitable for all ages and is designed to comply with the Google Play Families Policy, COPPA (USA), GDPR (EU) and KVKK (Türkiye). We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Links that leave the game are protected by a parental gate.

  8. Your rights

    You have rights of access, correction and deletion under GDPR and KVKK. Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is nothing for us to access or delete. You can contact us at the address above with any question.

  9. Changes

    We may update this policy. Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
